Research Interests

  • Radio astronomy, radio interferometry, instrumentation and techniques.
  • Phased arrays, calibration and array signal processing.
  • High speed DSP, reconfigurable computing, FPGAs.
  • Experimental and observational cosmology, 21-cm cosmology, precision radiometry, data analysis for 21-cm experiments, radio-sky modelling at low-frequencies.
  • All-sky spherical harmonic transit interferometry (m-mode imaging)
  • Numerical linear algebra, ill-posed inverse problems, iterative methods.
  • Ionospheric scintillation.
