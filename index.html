About Me

Hello, and welcome to my website. My name is Jishnu N. Thekkeppattu and I am a radio astronomer-engineer. I have a bachelors degree in electronics and communications engineering from Kannur University, Kerala, India and a PhD in physics from Curtin University, Australia, specialising in experimental radio astronomy. My PhD thesis was on low frequency radio cosmology, for which I designed, built and commissioned radiospectrometers as well as analyzed data from them.

My research interests are in radio astronomy, radio telescope system science, data reduction, high speed signal processing, and experimental cosmology. I translate novel ideas into practical systems with emphasis on system level design of hardware, software and firmware. I am also adept in mathematical modelling of radio telescope systems and radio data analysis. As an experienced radio amateur, I have hands-on expertise in RF systems engineering and electronics troubleshooting as well. Thank you for visiting my website !
